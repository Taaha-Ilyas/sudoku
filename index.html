Sudoku Duel

Race your friends
to the last digit.

Everyone gets the same puzzle. First to submit a correct grid wins — with up to 8 players, finishing places are ranked.

Create a room

You'll be the host and choose the difficulty.

Join a room

  • Rooms hold up to 8 players. Share the code or the invite link.
  • Nothing is checked while you play — conflicts aren't shown.
  • Press Submit when the grid is full. If it's wrong, one mistake is highlighted.
  • Keyboard: 1–9 to fill, Backspace to clear, arrows to move, N for notes, Ctrl+Z to undo.
